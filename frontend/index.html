About the Fair

Celebrating agriculture, trade and innovation

The Ghana National Food & Agribusiness Fair 2026 is a national platform that brings together stakeholders across the agricultural value chain to promote trade, innovation, collaboration and food security.

The fair showcases fresh farm produce, processed foods, agribusiness services, modern farming solutions and market opportunities for local and international participants.

About the Fair

Where agriculture meets markets and opportunity.

The Ghana National Food and Agribusiness Fair creates a platform where producers, processors, businesses, institutions and investors can meet, learn and build meaningful partnerships.

From farm production to processing, packaging, technology, financing and market access, the fair highlights the full agricultural value chain.

Connect Meet farmers, buyers, businesses and development partners.
Discover Explore products, innovations and market opportunities.
Grow Build partnerships that support sustainable agribusiness growth.

What to Expect

Three days of connections, learning and business opportunities

Experience Ghana's food and agribusiness value chain in one dynamic national event.

01

Agricultural Exhibition

Discover fresh produce, processed foods, agricultural inputs, technologies and locally developed products.

02

Business & Market Linkages

Connect producers and processors with buyers, retailers, investors and potential business partners.

03

Knowledge & Innovation

Learn from researchers, practitioners and agribusiness leaders about emerging opportunities and solutions.

04

Youth & Agribusiness

Inspire students and young entrepreneurs to explore agriculture as a modern and viable business.
